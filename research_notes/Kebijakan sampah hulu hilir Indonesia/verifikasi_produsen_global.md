## 1. Unilever: revisi target plastik 2024 dan pembaruan 2025–2026

**Status: [TERVERIFIKASI-PRIMER]**

**Sumber 1.** Unilever, "How we're aiming for greater impact with updated plastic goals", ditulis Pablo Costa (Global Head of Packaging), **terbit 30 April 2024**.
URL: https://www.unilever.com/news/news-search/2024/how-were-aiming-for-greater-impact-with-updated-plastic-goals/
Dibaca lewat arsip: https://web.archive.org/web/20240501092948/https://www.unilever.com/news/news-search/2024/how-were-aiming-for-greater-impact-with-updated-plastic-goals/

Kutipan:
- "First, we aim to reduce our virgin plastic use by 30% in 2026, and by 40% in 2028."
- "We've reduced our virgin plastic use by 18% against a 2019 baseline"
- "we also recognise that we've fallen short against some of our most ambitious goals … For example, we didn't just aim to reduce our use of virgin plastic, we wanted to halve it. This has proved more challenging than any of us anticipated at the time."
- "we will work to ensure that 100% of our plastic packaging is reusable, recyclable or compostable, by 2030 for rigid plastic, and by 2035 for flexibles." (Target lama untuk semua kemasan adalah 2025. Tenggat ini juga mundur.)
- "a gap remains between the 'technical' recyclability rate of our plastic packaging portfolio (72%) versus the 'actual' recyclability rate (53%)."
- "We're calling on governments to accelerate the implementation of mandatory and well-designed extended producer responsibility (EPR) schemes"

Catatan:
- Halaman ini tidak memakai angka "50%". Unilever menulis "halve it" (memangkas setengah). Tahun 2025 sebagai tenggat target lama tidak disebut eksplisit di halaman ini. Komitmen asli 2019 ("halve our use of virgin plastic" pada 2025) tercatat di media, misalnya [Business Standard/PTI, 7 Okt 2019](https://www.business-standard.com/article/pti-stories/unilever-announces-commitments-to-reduce-plastic-waste-to-halve-use-of-virgin-plastic-by-2025-119100700399_1.html) [CUPLIKAN].
- Media pada saat itu: [Packaging News AU, 22 Apr 2024](https://www.packagingnews.com.au/latest/unilever-s-realistic-plan-scales-back-plastic-targets) [TERVERIFIKASI-SEKUNDER].

**Sumber 2 (pembaruan 2025–2026).** Unilever, halaman "Plastics", https://www.unilever.com/sustainability/plastics/
Dibaca lewat arsip 20 Sep 2026: https://web.archive.org/web/20260920090423/https://www.unilever.com/sustainability/plastics/

Kutipan:
- "Reduce our virgin plastic footprint – by 30% by 2026, and 40% by 2028". Target 2024 masih berlaku. **Tidak ada revisi lanjutan hingga Sep 2026.**
- "We reduced our use of virgin plastics for our product packaging by 29% versus 2019. This represents a reduction of 6% compared to 2024"
- "57% of our plastic packaging was reusable, recyclable or compostable (75% rigids, 15% flexibles)."
- "We collected and processed more plastic than we sold, surpassing our target of 100%." Salah satu caranya: "Participating in extended producer responsibility schemes"
- "Hard-to-recycle flexible plastic packaging remains one of the consumer goods industry's biggest and most complex environmental challenges. We recognise our packaging is part of this problem."

Rumusan aman: *"Pada April 2024 Unilever mengakui gagal memenuhi janji memangkas separuh penggunaan plastik virgin dan menggantinya dengan target −30% pada 2026 dan −40% pada 2028 (dibanding 2019). Menurut laman Unilever sendiri, hingga 2025 pengurangan baru 29%, dan hanya 15% kemasan plastik fleksibelnya (termasuk sachet) yang dapat didaur ulang, dipakai ulang atau dikomposkan."*

## 2. Nestlé: target pengurangan plastik virgin 12–17% pada 2030

**Status: [TERVERIFIKASI-PRIMER]** (dokumen Nestlé) dan **[TERVERIFIKASI-SEKUNDER]** (Food Dive)

**Sumber primer.** Nestlé, *Packaging Sustainability Strategy*, **September 2026**, PDF 27 halaman, target ada di hlm. 10.
https://www.nestle.com/sites/default/files/2026-09/nestle-packaging-sustainability-strategy-en.pdf

Kutipan (hlm. 10):
- "By 2030, we aim to achieve 12%-17% virgin plastic reduction, from the 2018 baseline*"
- "Nestlé measures the reduction of virgin plastics usage from the 2018 baseline (611 kT excluding Nestlé Waters and Premium Beverages)."
- Catatan kaki 1: "In light of the announced future divestment, the target excludes Nestlé Waters and Premium Beverages. It is subject to change once the divestment is concluded, based on its impact on the packaging portfolio."
- Kontribusi yang diharapkan: plastik daur ulang 50–60%; reduksi dan eliminasi 30–40%; material alternatif dan guna ulang/isi ulang 10–15%.

**Sumber sekunder.** Maria Rachal, "Nestlé…", *Food Dive*, **29 Sep 2026** (terbit pertama di Packaging Dive).
https://www.fooddive.com/news/nestle-plastic-reduction-strategy-2030/831372/
- "by between 12% to 17% come 2030, from a 2018 baseline"
- Kemasan guna ulang hanya 0,51% pada 2025. Kandungan daur ulang sekitar 21% (2025).

**Target lama (pembanding).** Januari 2020: memangkas plastik virgin "by one third by 2025". Sumber: liputan [FoodNavigator, 17 Jan 2020](https://www.foodnavigator.com/Article/2020/01/17/Nestle-wants-to-lead-the-shift-from-virgin-plastics) dan [MarketScreener](https://ca.marketscreener.com/quote/stock/NESTLE-S-A-9365334/news/Nestle-S-A-creates-market-for-food-grade-recycled-plastics-launches-fund-to-boost-packaging-innov-29853044) [CUPLIKAN; siaran pers asli nestle.com 404].

Catatan hukum:
- Target 12–17% **tidak mencakup bisnis air minum Nestlé** dan bisa berubah setelah divestasi. Ini perlu disebut agar tidak dituduh salah kutip.
- Angka "pengurangan 28% pada 2025" yang beredar di sebagian media **tidak dipakai**, karena basisnya berbeda dan tidak terverifikasi.

Rumusan aman: *"Dalam strategi kemasan yang terbit September 2026, Nestlé hanya menargetkan pengurangan plastik virgin 12–17% pada 2030 dibanding 2018 (di luar bisnis air minumnya). Pada 2020 Nestlé pernah berjanji memangkas sepertiga pada 2025."*

## 5. PPWR UE (Regulasi (UE) 2025/40)

**Status: [TERVERIFIKASI-PRIMER]**

**Sumber.** Teks Jurnal Resmi UE, OJ L, 2025/40, **22.1.2025**. Salinan resmi Kantor Publikasi UE: http://publications.europa.eu/resource/cellar/d34251c8-d865-11ef-be2a-01aa75ed71a1.0006.03/DOC_1 (CELEX 32025R0040). URL EUR-Lex: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2025/40/oj (diblokir untuk akses otomatis).

Kutipan:
- Judul: "REGULATION (EU) 2025/40 OF THE EUROPEAN PARLIAMENT AND OF THE COUNCIL of 19 December 2024 on packaging and packaging waste…"
- **Pasal 71:** "This Regulation shall enter into force on the twentieth day following that of its publication in the Official Journal of the European Union. It shall apply from 12 August 2026. However, Article 67(5) shall apply from 12 February 2029."
- **Pasal 45(1), EPR wajib:** "Producers shall have extended producer responsibility under the schemes established in accordance with Articles 8 and 8a of Directive 2008/98/EC and with this Section for the packaging … that they make available for the first time on the territory of a Member State"
- **Pasal 6(8), eco-modulation wajib:** "…18 months from the date of entry into force of the delegated acts adopted pursuant to paragraph 4 … the financial contributions paid by producers in order to comply with their extended producer responsibility obligations as laid down in Article 45 **shall be modulated** in accordance with the recyclability performance grades"
- **Pasal 6(4):** delegated act tersebut harus diadopsi Komisi "By 1 January 2028".
- **Pasal 7(7), modulasi berdasarkan kandungan daur ulang bersifat opsional:** "…may be modulated based on the percentage of recycled content"

**Komisi Eropa** (https://environment.ec.europa.eu/topics/waste-and-recycling/packaging-waste_en): "In force since 11 February 2025"; "Application date: 12 August 2026" [TERVERIFIKASI-SEKUNDER via WebFetch atas laman resmi].

Catatan penting (jangan dilebih-lebihkan):
- Tanggal yang benar: **berlaku (entry into force) 11 Feb 2025; diterapkan (application) 12 Agu 2026.** Klaim "12 Agustus 2026" benar untuk tanggal penerapan.
- EPR kemasan **sudah wajib di UE sebelum PPWR** (Direktif 94/62/EC, tenggat 31 Des 2024, sesuai resital PPWR). PPWR menegaskan dan menyeragamkannya.
- Eco-modulation berbasis **kelas daur ulang (A/B/C)** bersifat wajib, tetapi **baru berlaku 18 bulan setelah delegated act** yang tenggatnya 1 Jan 2028, jadi sekitar 2029–2030, bukan sejak 12 Agu 2026. Di bawah Direktif Kerangka Sampah (Pasal 8a(4)(b)) modulasi biaya sudah diminta "where possible".
- Komisi juga menyebut usulan "Environmental Omnibus" (Des 2025) yang akan menangguhkan sebagian kewajiban perwakilan EPR. Status: masih dibahas.

Rumusan aman: *"Regulasi Kemasan UE (PPWR, Regulasi (UE) 2025/40) diterapkan sejak 12 Agustus 2026. Regulasi ini mewajibkan setiap produsen yang memasarkan kemasan di UE ikut skema EPR, dan iurannya wajib dibedakan menurut tingkat keterdaurulangan kemasan begitu kriteria teknisnya ditetapkan Komisi."*

## 6. Filipina: RA 11898 (Extended Producer Responsibility Act of 2022)

### 6a. Isi undang-undang

**Status: [TERVERIFIKASI-PRIMER]** (teks UU via Lawphil; Official Gazette 403)

**Sumber.** Republic Act No. 11898, 23 Jul 2022 (berlaku tanpa tanda tangan Presiden). Teks: https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2022/ra_11898_2022.html

Kutipan:
- **Sec. 44-C** (RA 9003 sebagaimana diubah): "Plastic packaging shall include the following: (a) **Sachets, labels, laminates and other flexible plastic packaging products, whether single layer or multi-layered with plastics or other materials**; (b) Rigid plastic packaging products… (c) Plastic bags… (d) Polystyrene."
- **Sec. 44-B:** obliged enterprises = "large enterprises that generate plastic packaging waste". UMKM dikecualikan, kecuali total aset semua entitas dengan merek yang sama melampaui batas usaha menengah.
- **Sec. 44-F**, target pemulihan jejak plastik tahun sebelumnya: "December 31, 2023 – twenty percent (20%); December 31, 2024 – forty percent (40%); December 31, 2025 – fifty percent (50%); December 31, 2026 – sixty percent (60%); December 31, 2027 – seventy percent (70%); and December 31, 2028 and every year thereafter – eighty percent (80%)."
- **Sec. 49(g)**, untuk yang gagal mendaftar (44-E) atau gagal memenuhi 44-F: "(1) a fine of not less than Five million pesos (P5,000,000.00) but not exceeding Ten million pesos … for the first offense; (2) … Ten million … Fifteen million … second offense; (3) … Fifteen million … Twenty million pesos (P20,000,000.00) for the third offense and automatic suspension of business permit". Jika target tidak tercapai: denda yang sama "or a fine twice the cost of recovery and diversion of the footprint or its shortfall, whichever is higher."
- **Sec. 44-G:** audit pihak ketiga independen. Laporan tersertifikasi "shall be made available to the public through the website of the Department", dengan pengecualian kerahasiaan dagang.

Catatan: denda ₱5–20 juta bertingkat per pelanggaran (pertama, kedua, ketiga), bukan satu rentang tunggal. Ada juga opsi denda dua kali biaya pemulihan.

### 6b. Apakah Unilever, Nestlé, Coca-Cola, Danone terdaftar sebagai obliged enterprise?

- Sungai Watch mengaudit **kemasan**, termasuk logam, kaca dan Tetra Pak. Pakai "item kemasan bermerek", bukan "sampah plastik", terutama untuk Nestlé.

Rumusan aman: *"Audit merek Sungai Watch 2024 atas 623.021 item kemasan yang dikumpulkan di Bali dan Banyuwangi menemukan 39.480 item dari Danone (peringkat 2), 32.372 dari Unilever (peringkat 4) dan 17.678 dari Nestlé (peringkat 8); Wings berada di peringkat pertama dengan 52.600 item."*

### Koreksi yang wajib masuk ke draf rilis
1. **PRAISE ≠ 2020.** Yang diluncurkan 2020 adalah IPRO.
2. **Coca-Cola**: rilis 2024 tidak memakai kata "menghapus". Tulis "tidak lagi mencantumkan". Target lama 50% berlaku untuk "packaging", bukan hanya rPET.
3. **Nestlé**: target 12–17% tidak mencakup bisnis air minum.
4. **PPWR**: eco-modulation berbasis kelas daur ulang berlaku 18 bulan setelah delegated act (≤1 Jan 2028), tidak langsung pada 12 Agu 2026.
5. **Filipina**: tidak ada daftar publik DENR. Jangan klaim Unilever atau Danone "terdaftar".
6. **Sungai Watch**: "item kemasan", bukan "sampah plastik"; cakupan Bali dan Banyuwangi, bukan nasional.
